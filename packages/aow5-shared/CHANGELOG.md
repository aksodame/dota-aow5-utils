# Game data changelog

What each Age of Weapons 5 workshop pak changed in the data this repo ships — the item table, its prices
and names, and the heroes and abilities the planner offers.

Generated by `private/actualize.mjs`; newest first. Entries are keyed to the pak they came out of rather
than to a release, because one tracker release can carry several and a pak that changed nothing gets no
entry at all. Names appear as the addon localizes them, so an item its authors have not translated yet is
in Chinese here too.
