end of it. It is about what stands behind it and does not go away with the fix.

**Thesis four: nothing will happen to them, and they know it.** That is exactly

to the administration by direct message cannot count on it being fixed, nor on

this attitude the report channel stops being a report channel and becomes a
source of advantage for a small circle.

visible at all — and they can affect the economy and the balance far more than
